Selected Work

Computer Vision / Medical AI

Dental OPG Cavity Detection

Dentists miss cavities. Not because they are bad at their jobs. Because they review dozens of X-rays under time pressure every single day. I built an object detection model that reads Orthopantomographic (full-mouth panoramic) X-rays and flags potential cavity regions in seconds. You upload a dental OPG scan. The model draws bounding boxes around suspicious areas with confidence scores. It works as a second pair of eyes. The kind of tool that could genuinely change outcomes in under-resourced clinics across Africa and beyond. Trained on annotated dental X-ray datasets using a fine-tuned YOLO architecture. Deployed as a live interactive app on HuggingFace Spaces.

Computer Vision / Medical AI

Kidney Condition Classifier

CT scans reveal everything. But reading them takes years of training and time that busy radiologists do not always have. I trained a multi-class deep learning classifier that categorizes kidney CT images into four conditions: Normal, Cyst, Stone, and Tumor. The model processes an image in under a second and returns a classification with a confidence score. This is medical AI that is actually useful. Not a research toy. A real tool that could assist radiologists in high-volume settings, flagging images that need urgent attention. Built on a ResNet architecture fine-tuned on a curated kidney CT dataset. Deployed as a live Gradio app on HuggingFace Spaces where you can upload any kidney CT slice and get an instant result.

"

If intelligence is a cake, the bulk of the cake is unsupervised learning, the icing on the cake is supervised learning, and the cherry on the cake is reinforcement learning.

Yann LeCun Chief AI Scientist, Meta · Turing Award 2018 · Inventor of Convolutional Neural Networks
"

Every time I read a paper saying deep learning cannot do X, I know deep learning will eventually do X. It just needs more data or a slightly different architecture.

Geoffrey Hinton Turing Award 2018 · Pioneer of Backpropagation and Deep Belief Networks · "Godfather of Deep Learning"
"

The biggest bottleneck in deploying machine learning is not the algorithms. It is the data. Clean, labelled, representative data is what separates a research demo from a working product.

Andrej Karpathy Former Director of AI, Tesla · Former Research Scientist, OpenAI · Creator of micrograd and nanoGPT
"

If we want machines to think, we need to teach them to see. Perception is the gateway to intelligence, and vision is the richest channel we have.

Fei-Fei Li Director, Stanford Human-Centered AI Institute · Creator of ImageNet · Former Chief Scientist of AI/ML, Google Cloud
"

The thing that makes deep learning powerful is that it discovers the representations automatically. You do not design the features. The network learns what to look for.

Yoshua Bengio Turing Award 2018 · Scientific Director, Mila Quebec AI Institute · Pioneer of Deep Learning Theory

A learning machine is any machine that can be programmed in such a way that the output of the machine changes as a result of experience.

Every master algorithm is evolution done faster and more directed. We are building the thing that built us, and making it better at building the next version.

Pedro Domingos Professor of Computer Science, University of Washington · Author of The Master Algorithm
