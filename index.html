About Me

Based in Kampala, UG

A data scientist who believes the most powerful thing you can do with data is make it understandable — and then make it actually work.

🎓 Master of Science — Data Science & Analytics Postgraduate · Distinction

I am Paul Sentongo, a Data Scientist and Machine Learning professional with a Master's degree in Data Science and Analytics. My work sits at the intersection of rigorous statistical thinking and hands-on engineering — I don't just build models, I build systems that keep working long after the notebook is closed.

I grew up in Uganda, where I saw first-hand how the gap between "knowing something is wrong" and "having the data to prove it — and fix it" costs lives and opportunities. That observation drove me to pursue data science not as an academic exercise but as a practical force for change. Today I build ML systems, craft interpretable visualisations for non-technical stakeholders, and mentor the next generation of African data scientists.

I am actively seeking roles and collaborations where deep technical skill meets meaningful impact — whether that is a senior data science position, an MLOps engineering role, or a research partnership. If you are building something that matters and need someone who can take it from idea to production, let's talk.
