About Me

👤 Your Photo Here Upload your image to replace this
Based in Kampala, UG

A data scientist who believes the most powerful thing you can do with data is make it understandable to everyone.

I grew up in Uganda, surrounded by problems that deserved better solutions. After studying statistics and computer science, I spent years obsessing over one question: how can we apply data science where it matters most?

Today I build machine learning systems, craft visualizations that speak to non-technical audiences, and write about the intersection of data, society, and Africa's digital future. I believe local context is the secret ingredient most global data science misses.

When I'm not in front of a screen, I'm likely hiking a hill, trying a new restaurant, or planning my next trip somewhere I've never been.
